# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Criticize the
rule, the pattern, or the code, never the person who proposed it.

## What that means here

This project is about telling people their work looks generated. That is an
inherently touchy thing to automate, and it puts a particular obligation on
how we talk to each other.

- Disagree with evidence. "This rule fires on my code, here is the file" is a
  contribution. "This rule is stupid" is not.
- A false positive report is not an accusation. Someone whose work got flagged
  is doing the project a favor by saying so.
- Do not use this tool, or its output, to disparage a specific person's work
  in an issue or a PR. Bring the pattern, not the byline.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or unwelcome attention of any kind.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to Corey Schuman at cschuman@gmail.com, or privately through
GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit, hide, or
delete comments and may block accounts, at his discretion and without
obligation to explain the decision publicly.

This is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
shortened to what a project this size can actually enforce.
